The first game

Tongits TwoBots

Three-player Tongits — you against two bots. Offline, no accounts, no ads.

In development · not released

Tongits is a Filipino card game: three players, a 52-card deck, melds and discards, and three ways a hand can end. The name is the pitch — the other two seats are bots, so a hand takes as long as you want it to and never waits on anyone.

  • doneThe engine. Full hands play out, all three endings reachable, chips zero-sum across 10,000 simulated hands, no seat advantage.
